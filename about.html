WELCOME TO MANGAN TAKO

A familiar warmth.
A new discovery.

We bring the food of the highlands right to your table. Mangan Tako is a celebration of Cordilleran cuisine, created around a simple idea: a meal can be a way to connect with a place and its culture.

From the smoky character of Etag to the bold heat of Binungor, our menu invites you to explore, share, and settle in. Whether these flavors remind you of home or introduce you to something new, there's a place for you here.
